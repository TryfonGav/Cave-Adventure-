# Expansion Manual Playtest Checklist

1. Start a new profile, select a difficulty, and confirm a save appears for that profile after autosave or returning to menu.
2. Descend to Frost Vaults and Toxic Mire. Step on ice and toxic mist to verify movement slow and HP/poison effects.
3. Open chests in different biomes and verify biome materials appear in inventory.
4. Talk to a shopkeeper, accept a quest, press J, and confirm progress appears in the quest log and HUD tracker.
5. Complete a fetch, kill, or reach quest and verify XP/item rewards are granted and persisted after reload.
6. Press R, craft a recipe, equip the crafted item, and confirm stat text changes in Inventory.
7. Level up, press K, unlock a prerequisite skill, and verify its behavior changes gameplay.
8. Fight Stone Golem and Mire Wyrm. Confirm phase messages, boss music hooks, unique loot, and achievement unlocks.
9. Open P stats screen after crafting, quest completion, and boss kills. Confirm totals update and persist.
